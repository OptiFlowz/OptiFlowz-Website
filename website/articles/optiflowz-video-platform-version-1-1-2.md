# OptiFlowz Video Platform Version 1.1.2: Custom Playback, Chapters, and Multilingual Subtitles
### 24. April 2026

![OptiFlowz Video Platform version 1.1.2](/platform-releases/optiflowz-video-platform-v1-1-2.webp)

> Version 1.1.2 focused on the heart of the platform: the viewing experience. A fully custom player introduced chapter-aware playback, while multilingual subtitle generation made videos useful to a much broader audience.

## A fully custom video player

> The platform moved to a fully custom player with controls and behavior designed around the wider product experience. Owning the interface gave us a stronger base for specialized viewing features and future integrations.

## Video chapters

> Videos could now be divided into clear chapters. Viewers could understand the structure of longer content, jump to a relevant section, and return to a precise topic without manually searching through the timeline.

## A playlist-style chapter panel

> Chapters could open beside the video in a playlist-style view. Keeping the chapter list visible made navigation faster and preserved context while the video continued playing.

## Automatic subtitles in most languages

> Subtitle generation expanded beyond English to support most languages. This reduced manual transcription work and made the same content easier to follow across international and multilingual audiences.

---

## A more capable viewing experience

> Version 1.1.2 connected custom playback, structured navigation, and multilingual accessibility in one focused release. Long-form content became easier to explore and more useful to more viewers.
