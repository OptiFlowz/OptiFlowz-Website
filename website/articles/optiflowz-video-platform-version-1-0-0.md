# OptiFlowz Video Platform Version 1.0.0: The Foundation
### 5. March 2026

![OptiFlowz Video Platform version 1.0.0](/platform-releases/optiflowz-video-platform-v1-0-0.webp)

> Version 1.0.0 marked the first public foundation of the OptiFlowz Video Platform. This release brought together the essential parts of a modern video experience: secure accounts, dependable playback, content discovery, and a homepage that helps every viewer find something relevant.

## A homepage built for discovery

> The initial homepage introduced dedicated **Recommended** and **Trending** sections. Recommended content gives viewers a more relevant starting point, while Trending highlights videos attracting attention across the platform.
>
> Together, these sections made the homepage more than a static library: it became the first step in the content-discovery journey.

## Reliable video playback with Mux

> Version 1.0.0 shipped with a Mux-powered video player and the playback controls viewers expect. The integration provided a dependable foundation for streaming and gave us room to expand the viewing experience in later releases.

## Secure authentication and access control

> User authentication and access control were included from day one. The platform could distinguish between signed-in and signed-out users and protect content intended for specific audiences.

> Dedicated **registration and login pages** completed the account journey, giving viewers a clear and consistent way to create an account and return to the platform.

## The first recommendation and trending algorithms

> The first version of our recommendation and trending logic connected the content library to the homepage. Even at this early stage, viewers could move beyond manually browsing the catalogue and discover videos based on platform activity and relevance.

---

## What version 1.0.0 made possible

> This release established the product foundation that every later update could build on: a secure account system, a scalable playback layer, and the first personalized discovery experience.
