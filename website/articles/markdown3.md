![African elephant](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/Special:FilePath/African_elephant.jpg)

African elephants are the largest land animals on Earth and can weigh several tons.  
Their trunks are incredibly versatile and are used for breathing, grabbing food, drinking water, and communicating.

![Red fox](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/Special:FilePath/Red_fox_detailed_photo.jpg)

Red foxes are highly adaptable mammals that can live in forests, grasslands, mountains, and even cities.  
They are known for their intelligence and their ability to hunt small animals with quick and precise movements.

![Emperor penguin](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/Special:FilePath/Emperor-cold_hg.jpg)

Emperor penguins are famous for surviving the harsh Antarctic winters.  
They gather together in large groups to conserve warmth and take turns protecting their eggs.

![Green sea turtle](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/Special:FilePath/Green_sea_turtle_portrait.JPG)

Green sea turtles spend most of their lives swimming across warm ocean waters.  
They migrate long distances and often return to the same beach where they were born to lay eggs.

![Barn owl](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/Special:FilePath/A_Barn_Owl.jpg)

Barn owls are nocturnal hunters that rely on exceptional hearing to locate prey in darkness.  
Their soft feathers allow them to fly almost silently, making them extremely effective predators.
