# OptiFlowz Video Platform Version 1.1.3: Theater Mode, Featured Playlists, and Admin Uploads
### 1. May 2026

![OptiFlowz Video Platform version 1.1.3](/platform-releases/optiflowz-video-platform-v1-1-3.webp)

> Version 1.1.3 improved both sides of the platform: viewers received a more immersive player and stronger playlist discovery, while administrators gained the first dedicated workflow for uploading videos.

## Theater mode

> Theater mode expanded the player into a wider, more immersive layout without requiring full screen. Viewers could give the video more attention while keeping the surrounding platform controls within reach.

## Featured playlists on the homepage

> Playlists could now be featured directly on the homepage. Platform owners gained a clear way to promote a course, series, or curated collection instead of relying only on individual featured videos.

## The first admin upload page

> A basic administrator page introduced direct video uploads without AI generation. This created a straightforward manual publishing route and established the foundation for more advanced content-management tools.

## Continued performance improvements

> Further page-load speed and performance work helped the player, featured playlist sections, and new administration area remain quick and responsive.

---

## Better viewing and the start of content operations

> Version 1.1.3 paired a more cinematic viewer experience with the platform's first dedicated administrative upload workflow, connecting content presentation with content operations.
