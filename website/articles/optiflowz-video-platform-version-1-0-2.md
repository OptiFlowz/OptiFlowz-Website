# OptiFlowz Video Platform Version 1.0.2: Likes, History, and Continue Watching
### 27. March 2026

![OptiFlowz Video Platform version 1.0.2](/platform-releases/optiflowz-video-platform-v1-0-2.webp)

> Version 1.0.2 made it easier for viewers to return to content and keep track of what matters to them. Reactions, watch history, and Continue Watching introduced the first persistent layer of personalization across the platform.

## Like and dislike feedback

> The new like and dislike system gave viewers a simple way to respond to a video. These signals added a useful feedback mechanism around content and created another input for future discovery improvements.

## Continue Watching on the homepage

> A dedicated **Continue Watching** section surfaced unfinished videos directly on the homepage. Viewers could resume where they left off without searching for the same content again.

## A more useful account page

> The account page brought together **liked videos, watch history, and Continue Watching**. This created one personal destination for revisiting saved activity and managing the viewing journey over time.

## Faster page loads

> Page-load speed and performance improvements made everyday navigation more responsive, especially as personalized sections began processing more viewer-specific data.

## Continued usability polish

> Further design and usability improvements clarified the new account and homepage sections while maintaining a consistent visual experience across the product.

---

## A platform that remembers the viewer

> Version 1.0.2 shifted the experience from one-time playback toward continuity. Viewers could react, leave, return, and quickly pick up their journey again.
