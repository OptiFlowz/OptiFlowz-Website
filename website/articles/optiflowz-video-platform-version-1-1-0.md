# OptiFlowz Video Platform Version 1.1.0: Profiles, Playlists, and Accessibility
### 10. April 2026

![OptiFlowz Video Platform version 1.1.0](/platform-releases/optiflowz-video-platform-v1-1-0.webp)

> Version 1.1.0 turned the platform into a more personal, organized, and accessible viewing experience. The release introduced richer profiles and playlists, expanded search, automatic English subtitles, and major performance and usability improvements.

## Profiles that feel personal

> Users could now edit their **account name, biography, and profile picture**. These additions gave every account a recognizable identity and created a stronger foundation for community and collaboration features.

## Playlists from creation to continuous viewing

> Playlists arrived with their own dedicated pages, along with options to save and share them. This made it easier to organize related videos into courses, series, resource collections, or personal watch lists.
>
> **Playlist autoplay** kept viewers moving through a collection without interruption, while the playlist remained visible beside the active video so the full sequence and upcoming content were always clear.

## Search across more than videos

> Search was expanded to include **playlists and people alongside individual videos**. Viewers could reach a creator, a curated collection, or a specific video from the same search experience instead of navigating separate parts of the platform.

## Automatic English subtitles

> Version 1.1.0 introduced automatic English subtitle generation. New uploads could become easier to follow without requiring a fully manual transcription workflow, improving both convenience and content accessibility.

## A more accessible interface

> Accessibility improvements made core interactions clearer and easier to navigate for a broader range of users. This work complemented subtitles and established accessibility as an ongoing part of the platform rather than a one-time addition.

## Faster loading and a polished experience

> Major page-load and performance improvements reduced friction throughout the platform. Further design and usability refinements made common actions more intuitive, while a new **homepage content slider** created a more flexible way to feature important videos and collections.

---

## A platform designed around the viewer

> Version 1.1.0 connected identity, organization, discovery, and accessibility. Viewers could personalize their presence, build and share collections, find more types of content, and move through the platform with less friction.
