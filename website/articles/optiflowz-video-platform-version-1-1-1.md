# OptiFlowz Video Platform Version 1.1.1: Stronger Playlists, Search, and Account Recovery
### 17. April 2026

![OptiFlowz Video Platform version 1.1.1](/platform-releases/optiflowz-video-platform-v1-1-1.webp)

> Version 1.1.1 strengthened the organization and account experience introduced in the previous release. Playlists became a more dependable way to package content, search reached more platform entities, and password recovery gave users a safe route back into their accounts.

## Playlist pages, saving, and sharing

> Playlists were supported as complete destinations with dedicated pages and clear saving and sharing actions. A curated sequence could be treated as its own resource rather than only a collection hidden inside an account.

## Playlist autoplay with visible context

> Autoplay moved viewers through a playlist continuously, while the full list remained visible beside the active video. This made the current position and upcoming content easy to understand throughout a series.

## Search across videos, playlists, and people

> Search improvements brought playlists and people into the same results experience as videos. Users could find a specific asset, a curated collection, or the person connected to it from one place.

## Password reset

> A dedicated password-reset flow gave users a secure way to regain access without administrator intervention, completing an important part of the account lifecycle.

## Continued performance work

> Additional page-load and performance improvements kept navigation responsive as playlists, expanded search results, and account flows increased the platform's scope.

## More dependable discovery and access

> Version 1.1.1 made organized content easier to consume and find while ensuring users had a clearer, safer way to recover their accounts.
