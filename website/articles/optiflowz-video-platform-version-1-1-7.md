# OptiFlowz Video Platform Version 1.1.7: Quizzes, Certificates, and Live Analytics
### 24. July 2026

![OptiFlowz Video Platform version 1.1.7](/platform-releases/optiflowz-video-platform-v1-1-7.webp)

> Version 1.1.7 expanded the OptiFlowz Video Platform from a content destination into a more complete learning and measurement system. Quizzes, courses, certificates, and live analytics now connect the viewer experience with tangible outcomes for learners and platform owners.

## Quizzes and courses

> Platform owners can organize learning into courses and reinforce key ideas with quizzes. Rather than presenting videos as isolated items, this structure supports a guided journey through related content and gives learners a clear sense of progression.

## Quiz certificates

> Certificates provide a shareable record of quiz achievement. They turn successful completion into a concrete outcome that can support internal training, professional education, and other learning programs where recognition matters.

## Live analytics at every level

> Version 1.1.7 introduced live analytics for **individual videos, channels, and the platform as a whole**. Teams can move from a detailed content view to a broader performance picture without piecing together separate reporting workflows.
>
> This layered approach makes analytics useful for both day-to-day content decisions and higher-level platform planning.

## Continued UI improvements

> Further interface improvements and polish refined the experience across the platform. Small details, clearer interactions, and more consistent presentation make the new learning and analytics features feel like a natural part of the product.

## Fully moved to Next.js

> The platform completed its move to Next.js, creating a stronger technical foundation for search-engine visibility and future development. The migration also gives the product a more unified architecture as the platform continues to grow.

---

## Connecting content to outcomes

> With version 1.1.7, teams can structure content into learning experiences, verify knowledge, recognize completion, and see performance as it happens. It is an important step toward a platform where publishing, learning, and measurement work together.
