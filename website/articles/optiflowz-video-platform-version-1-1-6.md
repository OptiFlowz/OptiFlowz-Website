# OptiFlowz Video Platform Version 1.1.6: In-App AI Guidance and the Next.js Migration
### 26. June 2026

![OptiFlowz Video Platform version 1.1.6](/platform-releases/optiflowz-video-platform-v1-1-6.webp)

> Version 1.1.6 combined a new user-facing assistant with an important architectural change behind the scenes. The platform became easier to navigate while the first stage of its Next.js migration established a stronger base for search visibility and future development.

## An AI assistant inside the platform

> A new AI assistant began guiding users through the app. Viewers could get contextual help with navigating the interface and understanding available actions without leaving the platform to search for instructions.

## Continued UI improvements

> Further interface improvements and polish refined common interactions across the product. This ongoing work helped the assistant feel integrated into a clear and consistent experience rather than added as a separate layer.

## The Next.js migration begins

> Version 1.1.6 began moving the platform to Next.js with better SEO as a central goal. Starting the migration in stages allowed the architecture to evolve while the existing product continued to serve viewers and administrators.

---

## Better guidance on a stronger foundation

> This release improved the immediate experience through in-app assistance while preparing the technical foundation completed in version 1.1.7. It connected practical user support with long-term platform growth.
