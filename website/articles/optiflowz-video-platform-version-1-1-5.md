# OptiFlowz Video Platform Version 1.1.5: A New Interface and Stronger Content Protection
### 5. June 2026

![OptiFlowz Video Platform version 1.1.5](/platform-releases/optiflowz-video-platform-v1-1-5.webp)

> Version 1.1.5 was a major visual and technical step forward. It refreshed the interface, introduced conversations directly on videos, simplified account access, strengthened content protection, and made the platform faster and clearer when services were unavailable.

## A major UI/UX redesign

> The platform received a broad interface redesign focused on clearer hierarchy, more consistent components, and smoother everyday navigation. The update brought the viewing and account experiences into one more cohesive design system.

## Comments on videos

> Viewers could now leave comments directly on video pages. This created a dedicated space for questions, feedback, and discussion without moving the conversation into a separate communication tool.

## Sign in with Google

> Google account login added a familiar and convenient way to access the platform. Users could authenticate with an account they already use, reducing the effort required to get started or return to protected content.

## DRM integration

> Digital rights management added another layer of protection for premium and restricted video. The integration strengthened control over how protected streams are delivered and supported platform owners with higher content-security requirements.

## A clearer unavailable state

> Version 1.1.5 introduced a dedicated screen for the rare moments when the server is unavailable. Instead of leaving users with a broken or ambiguous page, the platform can communicate the situation through a deliberate, branded experience.

## Faster page loads

> Page-load speed and broader performance improvements made navigation feel more responsive across the product. This work also supported the larger interface redesign by keeping the updated experience quick and fluid.

---

## Design, conversation, and trust

> Version 1.1.5 combined a more polished interface with stronger access and content protection. At the same time, comments made the viewing experience more interactive and gave communities a natural place to engage around each video.
