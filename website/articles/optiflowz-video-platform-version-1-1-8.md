# OptiFlowz Video Platform Version 1.1.8: Interactive Transcripts, Mini Player, and Privacy Foundations
### 30. August 2026

![OptiFlowz Video Platform version 1.1.8](/platform-releases/optiflowz-video-platform-v1-1-8-v2.webp)

> Version 1.1.8 makes videos easier to navigate, follow, and keep watching. Interactive transcripts, a floating mini player, more flexible subtitle styling, and continued interface polish improve the viewing experience, while the first GDPR compliance measures establish a stronger privacy foundation for the platform.

## Interactive video transcripts

> Viewers can now follow a video's spoken content through an interactive transcript with clickable timestamps. Selecting a timestamp takes the viewer directly to that moment, making it faster to revisit a specific explanation, scan longer videos, or find important information without searching through the timeline manually.

## A floating mini player

> Videos can keep playing in a floating mini player while viewers browse other pages. This creates a more continuous experience across the platform and lets users explore related content without interrupting what they are already watching.

## Customizable subtitles

> Viewers can adjust subtitle color, size, and background in the video player. These controls make captions easier to read across different videos, screens, and viewing preferences while giving each user more control over their experience.

## The first GDPR compliance measures

> Version 1.1.8 began introducing GDPR compliance measures across the platform. This phased work establishes the privacy foundation needed for broader regulatory compliance while the platform continues to evolve.

## Continued UI improvements

> Further interface improvements and polish refined interactions throughout the platform. Together with the new playback and accessibility options, these updates make everyday navigation feel clearer, smoother, and more consistent.

---

## A more flexible viewing experience

> Version 1.1.8 gives viewers more ways to engage with video content on their own terms. They can read along, jump to precise moments, continue watching while browsing, and adapt subtitles to their needs—all within a more polished and privacy-conscious platform.
