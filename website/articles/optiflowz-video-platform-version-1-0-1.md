# OptiFlowz Video Platform Version 1.0.1: Better Video Pages, Search, and Sharing
### 13. March 2026

![OptiFlowz Video Platform version 1.0.1](/platform-releases/optiflowz-video-platform-v1-0-1.webp)

> Version 1.0.1 expanded the first release with the tools viewers need to understand, discover, and share content. Video pages became more informative, search made the library easier to navigate, and related recommendations created a more continuous viewing experience.

## Complete video pages

> Every video received a full page with its **title, description, speakers, tags, and supporting details**. This gave viewers useful context before and during playback while creating a consistent place for each piece of content.

## Similar-video recommendations

> A basic related-content algorithm began presenting similar videos beside the current video. Viewers could continue exploring relevant material without returning to the homepage after every watch.

## Video sharing

> Built-in sharing made it easier to send a useful video to a colleague, learner, or community member. Each video page could now work as a destination that viewers could revisit and distribute directly.

## The first dedicated search experience

> Version 1.0.1 introduced a basic video-search algorithm and a dedicated search page. This gave growing content libraries a faster alternative to manual browsing and laid the foundation for broader search in later versions.

## Continued design improvements

> Further design and usability improvements refined navigation and made the new video-page and search experiences feel consistent with the rest of the platform.

---

## From playback to discovery

> With richer pages, search, recommendations, and sharing, version 1.0.1 connected individual videos into a content-discovery system rather than a collection of isolated players.
