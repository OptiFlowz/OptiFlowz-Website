# OptiFlowz Video Platform Version 1.2.0: Global Access, Stronger Security, and Smarter Publishing
### 16. September 2026

![OptiFlowz Video Platform version 1.2.0](/platform-releases/optiflowz-video-platform-v1-2-0.webp)

> Version 1.2.0 makes the platform more accessible to international audiences and gives teams more control over access and publishing. Localization in 40 languages, a more comprehensive role system, stronger account security, and new ways to publish and take notes improve everyday work across the platform.

## A more comprehensive role system

> An expanded role system gives teams more flexibility in how they organize access across the platform. Clearer responsibilities and more comprehensive roles help support the different people involved in managing, publishing, and watching content.

## Platform localization in 40 languages

> The platform interface is now available in 40 languages, making navigation and everyday tasks more approachable for a wider audience. More languages are planned as localization continues to expand.

## Smarter, more personalized recommendations

> Improved video recommendations make it easier for viewers to discover content that is relevant to them. A more personalized experience helps people find what to watch next and explore more of the platform's video library.

## Two-factor authentication

> Users can add another layer of protection to their accounts with two-factor authentication via email or an authenticator app. This provides an additional verification step when signing in and gives users a choice of authentication methods.

## Scheduled video publishing

> Videos can now be scheduled for publication at a chosen time. Teams can prepare content in advance and coordinate releases without needing to be online at the moment a video goes live.

## Timestamped video notes

> Viewers can capture thoughts at specific moments with timestamped video notes. Connecting a note to a point in the video makes it easier to keep an observation in context and revisit the relevant part later.

## Privacy and compliance

> Building on the privacy foundations introduced in version 1.1.8, this release completes the platform's planned compliance work for GDPR, CCPA, and PIPEDA. Privacy remains part of the platform's ongoing development alongside account security and access management.

## Continued interface improvements

> Further UI improvements and visual polish refine the experience throughout the platform. These updates accompany the new language, security, publishing, and note-taking features to make everyday interactions clearer and more consistent.
