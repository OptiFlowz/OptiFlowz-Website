# HealthPlus Has a New Website, Built by OptiFlowz
### 20. September 2026

![The HealthPlus website displayed on a laptop, with OptiFlowz and HealthPlus branding](/clients/healthplus/ClientHealthPlus.webp)

> We designed and built a new website for **HealthPlus**, an organisation dedicated to the professional development of healthcare workers through accredited education. The site brings its educational programmes, latest news, and team story together in one place.

## A website built around the education

HealthPlus combines theoretical learning with practical training, including ultrasound education. Its website needed to introduce that work clearly and give visitors a straightforward way to explore the available programmes.

The homepage puts that purpose first. A direct introduction to HealthPlus sits alongside clear links to the education catalogue and the organisation's story. News from the team and its training activities has a dedicated place, giving visitors a view of the work behind the programmes.

## A clear path from interest to information

We organised the site around five main areas: Home, News, Education, About, and Contact. Each has a specific role in helping visitors get to know HealthPlus and find the information relevant to them.

The education page brings the programmes together in a visual catalogue, with illustrations that distinguish the different areas of study. It also points visitors to the HealthPlus Instagram profile for new dates and registration information, connecting the website with the team's existing communication channel.

The About page adds context: how HealthPlus approaches teaching, why practical work matters, and how learning continues beyond the classroom. Photographs from educational activities help tell that story through the people involved.

## A distinctive look across the site

The visual direction combines deep blue backgrounds, bright yellow accents, and medical illustrations. Prominent buttons make the main actions easy to spot, while rounded cards and consistent typography connect the different pages.

The imagery has a practical role, too. Medical visuals introduce the subject areas, while photographs of training sessions show the human side of the organisation. Together, they give the site a recognisable identity rooted in what HealthPlus does.

## Bringing the project together

For us, this project was about giving HealthPlus a digital home where its work is easy to understand and explore. The finished website connects the introduction, programme catalogue, news, and contact information into a coherent experience for prospective participants.

We're pleased to share this collaboration and the website we built for the HealthPlus team.
